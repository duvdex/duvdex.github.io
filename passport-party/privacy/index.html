Passport Party

Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026.

Passport Party is a trivia and timing game from Duvdex LLC with on-device guest saves, live room sync and optional in-app purchases. This policy covers test and store builds and this privacy page. No app account is required.

What stays on your device

  • The nickname a player enters.
  • Guest progress such as score, passport stamps, seen stops, selected language and saved run state.
  • Local room details such as the most recent room code shown on the device.

Live multiplayer data

Creating or joining a Quick Match or private room sends gameplay data to Supabase to keep the participating devices in sync. This can include your nickname, generated player ID, room code, lobby, room status, answers, timing taps, stop results, score updates, route order and room history. Other participants can see the nickname, scores and shared match state presented by the game. Avoid sensitive information in nicknames and room sharing.

Optional purchases

RevenueCat loads optional tip or supporter products, validates purchases and maintains anonymous purchase status. It may process purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, purchase status and basic app and store metadata for app functionality, purchase support and validation.

Apple or Google handles your store account and payment details. Passport Party does not receive payment-card numbers or your store password.

Services, tracking and security

Supabase provides live lobby, room and match sync. RevenueCat provides purchase handling. The current app has no separate third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking, and does not collect precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

Service connections use encryption in transit. Providers may process data outside your country and receive technical request metadata such as IP addresses. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose service may process website request metadata. This page does not add advertising cookies, analytics scripts or external font requests.

Service policies: Supabase, RevenueCat, Google, Apple and GitHub.

Sharing a room

The private-room share button opens your system share sheet so you can send the room code through an app you choose. Passport Party does not receive or store the message you send through that share sheet.

Storage and deletion

Guest progress stays on your device until removed. Reset Guest Save deletes the stored nickname and local progress, but does not delete live room or match rows already written to the multiplayer backend. Removing the app can remove local app data; operating-system backups and store purchase records are managed separately.

To request access, correction or deletion of backend data associated with your nickname or generated player ID, contact support@duvdex.com. Include enough non-sensitive information to identify the relevant records. We may need ownership proof. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card details. We use support correspondence to handle the request. Some purchase, security or legal records may need to be retained. Deletion is not an automatic refund or a promise of immediate erasure from every service or backup. We respond as applicable law requires.

Children

Passport Party is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal data from children. Store eligibility, parental permission and purchase controls still apply. Contact us if you believe a child's information requires attention.

Contact

Contact Duvdex LLC at support@duvdex.com. We date revisions and update the policy when our information handling changes.
